# Product brief

## Purpose

`hafiy.my` is Nazirul Hafiy’s professional home on the web. It should quickly communicate who Hafiy is, what kind of work he does, and why a reader should trust him with complex delivery and operations work.

The site is not trying to be a large portfolio platform. It is a focused personal site: clear positioning, credible experience, selected writing, and a small set of public experiments.

## Primary audience

- Hiring managers and recruiters looking for project, operations, ICT, or delivery leadership experience.
- Potential collaborators who need someone to structure ambiguous work and coordinate execution.
- People interested in AI-native work, agentic AI, and project-management practice.
- Sarawak-focused readers who discover the linked public experiments.

## Positioning

Core message:

> Project & Operations Manager turning complex, multi-stakeholder work into structured execution and predictable outcomes.

Supporting themes:

- 12+ years across project delivery, operations, connectivity, marketing, and digital communities.
- Practical experience with government, enterprise, startup, ICT, connectivity, and infrastructure contexts.
- Comfortable translating ambiguity into plans, operating rhythms, stakeholder alignment, and delivery follow-through.
- Active interest in AI-native workflows and public digital tools.

## Current content model

### Homepage

The homepage is the fast scan:

- portrait and one-line professional pitch;
- email, LinkedIn, and GitHub links;
- compact experience preview;
- latest writing preview;
- public experiment links.

### Experience page

The experience page carries the credibility detail:

- Independent Consultant work from December 2021 to August 2025;
- Telekom Malaysia roles from January 2013 to November 2021;
- metrics and outcomes around user growth, community feedback, connectivity project delivery, activation backlog reduction, and campaign operations.

### Writing

Writing currently focuses on AI-native work and project execution:

- `Build to Learn`
- `What Becoming AI-Native Means to Me`
- `From Building the Thing to Directing the Build`
- `Agentic AI as a Force Multiplier for Project Work`

The writing should stay personal, practical, and tied to how work actually gets planned, coordinated, shipped, and improved.

### Experiments

Current linked experiments:

- `PCDS 2030 Project Tracker` at `pcds2030.com`
- `SDE 2030 Blueprint Explorer` at `sde2030.com`
- `AI.Sarawak.News` at `ai.sarawak.news`

These links demonstrate public, Sarawak-aware digital exploration without turning the portfolio into a full product directory.

## Product principles

- Clarity over volume. A visitor should understand the site within seconds.
- Credibility over hype. Use specific outcomes and context instead of broad claims.
- Static over complex. Keep the maintenance burden low.
- Editorial over flashy. Writing and professional signal matter more than visual novelty.
- Public-proof where useful. Link to real experiments and profiles, but do not overload the homepage.

## Non-goals

- Do not turn the site into a full resume builder, blog engine, dashboard, or CMS.
- Do not add analytics, tracking, forms, or third-party scripts without a clear reason.
- Do not introduce a theme toggle merely because the site supports dark mode automatically.
- Do not expand into many project case studies unless the new content is polished and clearly strengthens the professional narrative.

## Success signals

- The homepage explains Hafiy’s professional value in one scan.
- Recruiters or collaborators can find contact links immediately.
- Experience details are specific enough to establish trust.
- Writing feels current and aligned with the AI-native/project-execution positioning.
- The site remains easy to edit by hand.
