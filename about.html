About

I bring order to complicated work.

I’m Nazirul Hafiy, a Project & Operations Manager based in Kuching, Sarawak.

Background

Over the past 12+ years, I’ve delivered programs across government agencies, enterprise environments, and international startups. The settings changed; the work stayed grounded in the same thing: helping people move from ambiguity to reliable execution.

At Telekom Malaysia, I spent close to nine years progressing from campaign management into regional project leadership. I delivered multi-million ringgit connectivity and ICT initiatives across rural and urban Sarawak, coordinating cross-functional teams, vendors, and stakeholders.

More recently, I worked independently with US-based startups on digital campaign operations, community engagement, product feedback, and quality assurance.

I’m PMP-trained and particularly interested in work where delivery, technology, and public impact meet.

How I work

  • Make the work visible. Clear owners, milestones, risks, and decisions beat heroic last-minute effort.
  • Translate across teams. Good delivery depends on technical, operational, and business people understanding one another.
  • Fix the system, not only the symptom. A solved issue is useful; a better workflow keeps paying off.
