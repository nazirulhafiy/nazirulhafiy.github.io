# hafiy.my Redesign

Personal website for Nazirul Hafiy.

This site is being redesigned as an editorial operating profile: a calm, credible, structured personal website that communicates Hafiy’s work across project delivery, operations, AI adoption, and public-sector systems.

## Core Direction

The website should feel like:

- a professional operating profile
- an editorial personal site
- a structured project delivery portfolio
- a public-sector innovation profile
- a home base for practical AI and digital systems work

It should not feel like:

- a generic AI consultant website
- a SaaS startup landing page
- a flashy portfolio
- a resume dumped onto a webpage
- a cyberpunk or over-animated AI website

## Positioning

Nazirul Hafiy works at the intersection of project delivery, operations, AI adoption, and public-sector systems — helping turn complex initiatives into structured execution, useful tools, and clearer decisions.

## Primary Audience

The website is mainly for:

- government leaders
- AI and digital transformation teams
- public-sector innovation stakeholders
- collaborators
- potential clients or employers
- people interested in practical AI adoption and project execution

## Website Goals

The site should help visitors quickly understand:

1. who Hafiy is
2. what he works on
3. why his background is credible
4. what kinds of problems he helps solve
5. what experiments or tools he is building
6. how to contact him

## Suggested Site Sections

1. Hero
2. What I Do
3. Operating Principles
4. Selected Work
5. Experience Timeline
6. Labs / Experiments
7. Writing / Notes
8. Contact

## Build Rules for AI Agents

Before making changes, read:

- `README.md`
- `docs/strategy.md`
- `docs/design.md`
- `docs/content.md`
- `docs/tasks.md`
- `docs/qa.md`

When editing:

- follow `docs/design.md`
- preserve the site direction
- do not introduce generic AI visuals
- do not rewrite unrelated files
- make small, reversible changes
- keep the site responsive
- maintain accessibility
- explain what changed after each task

## Redesign Principle

The AI agent is not the source of truth.

The documents are the source of truth.

The AI agent should execute against the documents.
